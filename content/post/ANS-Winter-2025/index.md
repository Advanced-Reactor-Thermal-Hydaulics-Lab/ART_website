At the 2025 ANS Winter Meeting in Washington, DC, ART member Ryan Hogg presented on "RGB-based Two-phase Flow Classification Method Toward IAC Treatment in TRACE".

{{< figure src="ryan_ANS.jpg" title="Graduate student Ryan Hogg presents his research" numbered="false" lightbox="true" >}}

Additionally, Preston Kilzer presented a poster summarizing the work ART lab has performed over the "Hydrodynamics of Two-Phase Flow under the Geometric Effects of Pipe Orientation and U-bends". This work is supported by the Department of Energy through the Nuclear Energy University Program (NEUP).
