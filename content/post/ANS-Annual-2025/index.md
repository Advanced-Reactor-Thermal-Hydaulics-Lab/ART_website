At the 2025 ANS Annual Conference in Chicago, IL, ART Lab had two papers presented. 

Alumni Adam Dix presented his work on "Exploratory Experiments for Local Parameter Variations in Inclined Two-phase Flows".

Alicja Stoppel presented her work on "Analyzing Two-phase Frictional Pressure Losses in Horizontal and Vertical Straight Pipes".

At the conference, current lab members were also able to catch up with alumni. Featured in the picture below in the front row from left to right are Dr. Kim, Grace O'Neil, David Kang, and Alicja Stoppel. In the back row featured are Drew Ryan, Parker Smith, Adam Dix, Preston Kilzer, Lauren Middleton, and Ryan Hogg.
